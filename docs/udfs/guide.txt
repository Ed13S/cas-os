=== DISC ===
-- What it does --
Discriminant b²-4ac of a quadratic. Tells you how many real roots there are.
-- Example --
Find the discriminant of x²-5x+6
-- Type --
disc(1,-5,6)
-- Gives --
1
-- Why --
b²-4ac = 25-24. Positive: two roots, zero: one, negative: none.

=== QROOT1 ===
-- What it does --
The root of ax²+bx+c using +√ in the quadratic formula.
-- Example --
Find the root of x²-5x+6 that uses +√
-- Type --
qroot1(1,-5,6)
-- Gives --
3
-- Why --
(5+√1)/2 = 3.

=== QROOT2 ===
-- What it does --
The root of ax²+bx+c using -√ in the quadratic formula.
-- Example --
Find the root of x²-5x+6 that uses -√
-- Type --
qroot2(1,-5,6)
-- Gives --
2
-- Why --
(5-√1)/2 = 2.

=== VERTX ===
-- What it does --
x-coordinate of the turning point of y=ax²+bx+c.
-- Example --
Turning point x of y=x²-5x+6
-- Type --
vertx(1,-5)
-- Gives --
2.5
-- Why --
-b/2a = 5/2.

=== VERTY ===
-- What it does --
y-coordinate of the turning point of y=ax²+bx+c.
-- Example --
Turning point y of y=x²-5x+6
-- Type --
verty(1,-5,6)
-- Gives --
-0.25
-- Why --
c-b²/4a = 6-6.25.

=== ZSCORE ===
-- What it does --
Converts a value to a z-score.
-- Example --
An IQ of 130, mean 100, SD 15. Find z
-- Type --
zscore(130,100,15)
-- Gives --
2
-- Why --
(130-100)/15 = 2.

=== XFROMZ ===
-- What it does --
Converts a z-score back into a value.
-- Example --
z = 2, mean 100, SD 15. Find x
-- Type --
xfromz(2,100,15)
-- Gives --
130
-- Why --
100 + 2x15 = 130.

=== BINMEAN ===
-- What it does --
Mean (expected value) of a binomial distribution.
-- Example --
X ~ Bin(10, 0.5). Find E(X)
-- Type --
binmean(10,0.5)
-- Gives --
5
-- Why --
np = 10x0.5.

=== BINSD ===
-- What it does --
Standard deviation of a binomial distribution.
-- Example --
X ~ Bin(10, 0.5). Find SD(X)
-- Type --
binsd(10,0.5)
-- Gives --
1.5811
-- Why --
√(np(1-p)) = √2.5.

=== RSF ===
-- What it does --
Rounds x to n significant figures.
-- Example --
Round 123456 to 2 significant figures
-- Type --

-- Gives --

Keeps 2 significant digits, the rest become zeros.

=== RANGE_ ===
-- What it does --
Projectile range on level ground. Angle in degrees, g = 9.8.
